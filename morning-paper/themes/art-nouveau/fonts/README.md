# art-nouveau — Font Downloads

All fonts are released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1).
Download the `.woff2` files and place them in this directory.

## Cinzel Decorative

An elaborately ornamented all-caps display font inspired by classical Roman
inscriptions, perfect for Art Nouveau mastheads and decorative headings.

- Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel+Decorative
- License: OFL-1.1
- Files needed:
  - `CinzelDecorative-Regular.woff2`
  - `CinzelDecorative-Bold.woff2`

## Cinzel

The non-decorative companion to Cinzel Decorative. Used for section titles
and story headlines where elegance is needed without excessive ornamentation.

- Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel
- License: OFL-1.1
- Files needed:
  - `Cinzel-Regular.woff2`
  - `Cinzel-Bold.woff2`

## IM Fell English

A digitization of a historical typeface cast by Dr. John Fell in the 17th century.
Its warm, slightly irregular letterforms suit the handcrafted Belle Époque aesthetic.

- Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IM+Fell+English
- License: OFL-1.1
- Files needed:
  - `IMFellEnglish-Regular.woff2`
  - `IMFellEnglish-Italic.woff2`

## Note

The renderer will warn (but not fail) if font files are absent.
System serif fallbacks (Georgia, Times New Roman) will be used instead.
